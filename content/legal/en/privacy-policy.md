# Privacy Policy

This page explains how BlueArchive Tools handles personal information and OAuth sign-in data.

Last updated: 2026-03-22

## Information we collect

- During OAuth sign-in such as Google or Discord, we may receive the minimum required account information from identity providers, including user ID, email address, and display name.
- We may store profile settings and feature-related preferences in Supabase to provide account features.

## How we use the information

- To keep you signed in
- To save profile settings and quick setup progress
- To prevent abuse and respond to support requests

## Sharing with third parties

- We do not share personal information with third parties without consent, except when required by law.

## Data deletion and contact

- If you want to unlink your account or request data deletion, please contact us through the guidance provided on this site.
